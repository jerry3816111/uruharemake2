# Annotation policy

Every record must distinguish:

- `observed`: directly visible utterance, action, timestamp, or event;
- `self_reported`: public statement by the target about the target;
- `inferred`: researcher or model interpretation with alternatives and confidence.

Hidden intention, private emotion, diagnosis, unpublished history, and performer identity remain unknown unless explicitly and lawfully observed. A public persona record must be a researcher paraphrase with source ID and locator; raw media, copied transcript, fixed target reply, and model output must not enter the tracked annotation dataset unless separately authorized.

Ambiguous behavior may have multiple acceptable labels or a target distribution. Coder and reviewer disagreement must be retained. Unfavorable, ambiguous, or failed records may not be removed to improve scores.

Behavior taxonomy, inclusion/exclusion rules, sample count, and adjudication policy are research-planner decisions and must be frozen before formal coding or model execution.
