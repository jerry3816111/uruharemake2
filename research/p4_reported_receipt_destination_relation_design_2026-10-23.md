## Before and causal boundary

The saved f2 User report was `友人は2089年4月19日に陶器の羅針盤を受け取る場所を紫の工房の南扉に決めたらしい。`. Its final visible reply was `友人は2089年4月19日に紫の工房の南扉で陶器の羅針盤を受け取る場所を決定したらしいんだね。`. The location marked by `に` in the source is the selected **receiving destination**, but `で` in the reply makes it read as a **decision site**. The saved private trace shows M31 `literal_summary_jp` already contains `南扉で…場所を決定`; M32 builds its `response_jp` from that summary; M39 accepts it unchanged; `visible_language_guard.changed=false`; P4-Z reports `source_pattern_unavailable`. Thus the first known role drift is at M31; M32 and later checks fail to detect it. This is a read-only diagnosis, not a new f2 run.

Before writing product code, a zero-model call to the existing `preserve_source_bound_proposition_p4` on the new r0 source and a same-shape wrong-role candidate returned `source_pattern_unavailable`, `abstain_unchanged`, and the `北廊下で銅の望遠鏡` wrong-role span was still visible (1/1). This is a controlled mechanism gap, not fresh model failure evidence.

## One core variable and scope

Only extend the existing product-only P4-Z source-bound proposition preservation for an exact, unquoted Japanese third-party hearsay grammar of `ACTORはDATEにOBJECTを受け取る場所をDESTINATIONに決めたらしい`. Use a full-string parse with bounded nonempty slots, real calendar date, and rejection of quote/question/first-person/extra-clause forms. Extract actor/date/object/destination and the typed relation **destination of receiving** from the current User source. For this grammar only, require a full-output structural match to a single positive reported-decision clause; mere slot substrings or a correct clause inside a quote, negation, or contradictory sentence are insufficient. Repair a relation drift with one short colloquial Japanese acknowledgment directly from the source slots. Do not use M31's candidate to fill a missing source slot. If the grammar or a protected boundary is not established, abstain rather than manufacture the intended destination. Preserve the already working P4-Z families and frozen M31/M32 implementation, model prompt, data, baseline, scoring, D action, renderer topology, memory policy, and product entry.

The source itself leaves the **date's attachment** (decision day versus receiving day) ambiguous; the repair must retain source order and must not claim a resolved temporal scope. It must not convert a `で` decision-site report into a selected destination or turn a quotation/question/direct first-person statement into third-party hearsay. The current P4-Z wrapper repairs **after** its captured pre-Z guard chain (P4-T/V/W and the base Japanese guard). For this new family, repair the incoming candidate first, then invoke the captured chain **exactly once**; never run the chain twice merely to check the output. Re-audit the exact returned string for actor/object/date/hearsay/destination relation, and bind P4-Z `after_digest` and the base guard's `final_reply_sha256` to the same final string. If a render/audit/guard step fails or a later wrapper changes it into a role error, terminate the turn before utterance/episode publication; recording `repair_failed_closed` while returning an unchecked proposition is forbidden. The current-cycle RightBrain speech history is written before the guard: when the visible reply changes, rebind only the exact matching current assistant row and its variant counters to the final reply, or terminate without publishing; never rewrite older rows or facts. Existing P4-Z families retain their old ordering and must be byte-identical. The trace may expose typed field digests and `temporal_scope=unresolved`, never raw source/reply or a factive psychological claim. A source-bound surface repair is not evidence that M31 now understands the proposition correctly.

Allowed implementation files after this design is committed: `uruha_source_bound_proposition_preservation_p4.py`, a new focused `test_p4_reported_receipt_destination_relation.py`, and only a necessary graph/entry test if the existing graph misses the trace. No change to `uruha_brain_mac.py`, formal M modules, old datasets/results, current D authority, or a special case for the saved f2 nouns/date. `CURRENT_TASK.md` and an acceptance report may record outcome.
