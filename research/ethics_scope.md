# Ethics and claim boundary

The target is a model of publicly observable behavior, not the complete private person. The project does not claim affiliation with, endorsement by, identity with, or consciousness of 一ノ瀬うるは or any other real person.

Internal variables are computational hypotheses. Public access is not training permission. Source rights, platform terms, storage boundaries, and provenance must be reviewed separately. Private life, medical inference, performer identity, undisclosed childhood, and hidden mental state are outside scope.

The system may support scientific reconstruction and probabilistic prediction only to the degree demonstrated by strict temporal evaluation. It must not be presented as perfect future prediction, mind reading, biological brain replication, or a substitute for the real person.
