## Gate verdict and limits

**Narrow product-turn cost attribution: PASS for these two isolated turns. Whole teacher-demo/full-product gate: strict FAIL.** This result adds a concrete per-turn provider/token/vector receipt tied to actual Japanese utterance, native runtime graph and private writeback; before, this product Web path attached no compute ledger. It does not establish broad conversation quality or advantage over a strong LLM. The cost card shows only instrumented scoped generation/vector operations. Eleven embedding-expected operations are **not** embedding token counts; speech/TTS, startup/background, CPU/RSS/energy, long-session ledger retention and complete production cost remain unmeasured. The two ~1.08 MB private log rows also make storage/retention a real cost to resolve, not a production-ready property.

Native Safari AX and live screenshots were observed, but the documented computer-use API provided no local-save operation for those pixels/AX. Thus the saved-native-visual gate remains **FAIL**; the private JSONL/graph projection is not a substitute for a durable native screenshot. The graph cost node was accessible in the actual Safari node graph, while its dense full-canvas layout did not yield a saved, self-explanatory teacher visual. No result was replayed or rescored after observation. Next necessary work should be a separately frozen, single-variable product item addressing one of the remaining cost/storage/visual delivery gates; formal same-model/human/temporal evaluation and integrated VRM/Function Calling remain separately pending.
