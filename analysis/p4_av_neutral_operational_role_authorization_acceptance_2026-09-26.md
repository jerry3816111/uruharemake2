# P4-AV neutral operational-role authorization: offline PASS

P4-AV passed its prospectively frozen offline gate with zero correction batches. The frozen dataset and contract were committed at `3c5437d` before implementation.

## What changed

M53 already distinguishes exact source labels and a very small set of structural headings from unsupported quoted categories. P4-AV adds one bounded, compositional Japanese grammar for workflow roles:

- a time/sequence position such as now, next, or later;
- an operational verb such as handle, check, or organize;
- a structural noun such as item, content, or thing;
- or a hold field/column.

These labels describe where an item sits in a temporary procedure. They do not assert what the item is, how important it is, what emotion caused it, what diagnosis applies, what the user secretly prefers, or whether the user can complete it.

The implementation is additive. It does not rewrite the plan or candidate, change scores or order, bypass M46 review, weaken M45/M39, alter a prompt/schema, add a model call, write factual memory, persist raw labels in the trace, or directly change the visible reply.
