## Conclusion and next design decision

The B4 failure was false self-attribution despite a handed direct User source. On new C cases, the bound gate genuinely prevented that narrow failure: it recognized the User-reported owner, rejected a third-party quote and unknown dates, exposed a two-place conflict, and abstained after correction. Yet the **whole C batch is strict FAIL**, because C0/C6/C8 direct statements still get a generic request for more detail, C3 naturalness is not established, the graph is not presentation-grade/self-explanatory, and saved native visual evidence, independent per-turn DB snapshots and full-cost measurements are absent. Lower-level 66/66 tests and 6/6 narrow actor outcomes do not override these failures.

The next single-variable design review should target **direct-User decision acknowledgment with authenticated paraphrase/actor lineage**, not reopen the now-frozen B/C prompts or relax their gold. This must use new source-disjoint cases, protect quotes/third-party/Uruha self-speech and final guard/writeback; keep graph presentation, visual capture and cost accounting as separate acceptance work. This result still cannot establish superiority over a strong same-model LLM, human felt-understanding, temporal generalization, personhood, or completion of UruhaBrain's long-term Goal.
